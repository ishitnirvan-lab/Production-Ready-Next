# Easter Egg: Hidden Love Letter 💌

Add a hidden Easter egg called **"The Secret Love Letter"** that perfectly matches the website's lovely, warm, and magical theme.

## Concept

Hide a tiny sealed love letter somewhere on the website. It should be subtle enough that only curious users will discover it while exploring.

## Trigger

* Place a small envelope icon in a hidden location that blends naturally with the design.
* It should not interfere with any buttons or content.
* The envelope should have a gentle floating animation to make it feel alive.
* On hover, it should glow softly and emit tiny sparkles.

## Opening Animation

When the user clicks the envelope:

* The screen slightly dims.
* The envelope floats to the center of the screen.
* It slowly opens with a smooth paper-unfolding animation.
* Soft hearts, sparkles, and flower petals gently float around.
* A beautiful handwritten-style letter appears.

## Love Letter

Display the following message:

> **To the wonderful soul exploring this little world, ❤️**
>
> Every click, every smile, and every moment you spend here makes this place feel a little more magical.
>
> Thank you for taking the time to discover the hidden corners of this experience.
>
> May your days always be filled with kindness, laughter, warm memories, and people who truly care about you.
>
> Sometimes the smallest surprises carry the biggest feelings.
>
> **With lots of love,**
>
> **A Hidden Friend 💌**

## Finishing Animation

After the user finishes reading:

* The letter gently folds itself closed.
* It transforms into a glowing heart.
* The heart floats upward and fades into tiny sparkles.
* Show the message:

**✨ "You discovered a hidden piece of love."**

## Achievement

Unlock:

**🏆 Secret Love Letter Found**

Save the achievement in `localStorage` so it remains unlocked on future visits.

## Bonus Features

* The letter should only appear once per user unless they reset their Easter egg progress.
* Add a **Replay Letter** option in the Easter Egg Collection after it has been discovered.
* Include a **Close** button so users can exit the animation at any time.
* Ensure the animation is responsive and works smoothly on both desktop and mobile devices.
* Keep all colors, fonts, and animations consistent with the existing lovely theme of the website.

The entire experience should feel emotional, magical, elegant, and rewarding—as if the visitor has discovered a heartfelt message hidden just for them.
