# Easter Egg: Bloom the Garden 🌸

Add a hidden Easter egg called **"Bloom the Garden"** that feels peaceful, magical, and full of love. It should blend naturally with the website's lovely aesthetic and reward curious users with a beautiful interactive experience.

## Concept

Hide a **small empty flower pot** somewhere on the website. It should look like a decorative part of the page so only observant users discover it.

## Trigger

* The flower pot should be small, cute, and blend into the existing design.
* On hover, it should gently sway or sparkle, hinting that it's interactive.
* Clicking the flower pot starts the mini experience.

## Gameplay

The user helps a flower grow through simple interactions.

### Stage 1 – Plant the Seed 🌱

* The empty pot appears in the center of the screen.
* A tiny seed gently falls into the pot with a soft animation.
* Display:
  **"Every beautiful journey begins with a tiny seed."**

### Stage 2 – Water the Plant 💧

* A cute watering can appears.
* The user clicks or taps the watering can **5 times** to water the seed.
* With each click:

  * Water droplets fall.
  * The soil sparkles.
  * Gentle nature sounds play (optional and muted by default).
  * A progress indicator fills.

### Stage 3 – Watch It Grow 🌿

The plant grows through smooth animated stages:

* 🌱 Tiny sprout
* 🌿 Small stem with leaves
* 🌷 Flower bud
* 🌸 A beautiful blooming flower
* 💖 The flower finally blossoms into a **heart-shaped flower** with glowing petals.

Each stage should feel smooth and magical with subtle sparkles.

## Celebration

Once the flower fully blooms:

* Butterflies gently fly around the flower.
* Flower petals drift across the screen.
* Soft glowing hearts float upward.
* The flower emits a warm golden glow.

Display the message:

**🌸 "Love grows with every little moment."**

## Reward

Unlock the achievement:

**🏆 Garden of Love**

Save the achievement in `localStorage` so it remains unlocked on future visits.

## Bonus Features

* The completed flower can remain as a decorative element for the rest of the session.
* Add a **Replay Bloom Animation** option in the Easter Egg Collection after it has been unlocked.
* Use smooth CSS animations and lightweight JavaScript only.
* Ensure the experience is fully responsive on desktop and mobile.
* Do not affect the existing layout or functionality of the website.

## Design Style

* Soft pastel pinks, whites, greens, and warm golden highlights.
* Rounded, cute illustrations.
* Gentle animations with no sudden movements.
* The experience should feel calming, wholesome, and magical, as if the user has secretly nurtured a little piece of love hidden within the website.
