# Add 5 Hidden Easter Eggs to My Website

I want to add **5 cute, hidden Easter eggs** to my website. The overall theme is **lovely, romantic, warm, and wholesome**. The Easter eggs should feel magical and rewarding—not distracting or annoying.

## General Rules

* Keep all existing website functionality unchanged.
* Use only HTML, CSS, and JavaScript/TypeScript already used in the project.
* No external libraries.
* All animations should be smooth (60 FPS if possible).
* Mobile and desktop compatible.
* Use soft pastel colors that match the existing theme.
* Sound effects should be optional and muted by default.
* Each Easter egg should be hidden and only activate when its trigger is discovered.
* Save completed Easter eggs in localStorage so users don't lose progress after refreshing.

---

## 1. Love Letter Hunt 💌

Hide **5 tiny envelope icons** across different pages or sections of the website.

Requirements:

* Envelopes should blend naturally into the design.
* Clicking one should play a small sparkle animation.
* Show progress like "2/5 Love Letters Found".
* After collecting all 5, open a beautiful animated love letter with a heartfelt message.
* Add soft floating hearts while the letter is open.
* Mark the achievement as completed.

---

## 2. Catch the Butterflies 🦋

Hidden trigger:
A secret keyboard word (for example: `butterfly`) or another hidden interaction.

Gameplay:

* 8–10 butterflies appear and gently fly around the screen.
* The player catches them by clicking or tapping.
* Butterflies should move naturally with curved paths.
* Game lasts about 20 seconds.
* After catching all butterflies:

  * Play a sparkle animation.
  * Show the message:
    **"You caught every little moment. ❤️"**

---

## 3. Heart Collector 💖

Hidden trigger:
A secret click combination or keyboard code.

Gameplay:

* Small glowing hearts float upward across the screen.
* Player collects 20 hearts.
* Hearts should gently wiggle while floating.
* Each collected heart creates a tiny pop animation.
* After completion:

  * Confetti made of hearts appears.
  * Show:
    **"Your heart is full of love."**

---

## 4. Bloom the Garden 🌸

Hidden trigger:
Click a small flower pot hidden somewhere on the page.

Gameplay:

* User repeatedly clicks or taps the flower pot to water it.
* Seed → Sprout → Leaves → Bud → Bloom animation.
* Final flower should bloom into a heart-shaped flower.
* Add butterflies and sparkles after blooming.
* Show:
  **"Love grows with every little moment."**

---

## 5. Heart Chase 💞

Hidden trigger:
A hidden heart icon.

Gameplay:

* A cute little heart appears.
* It playfully avoids the cursor but never too fast.
* After several attempts, it lets the player catch it.
* When clicked:

  * It grows slightly.
  * Small hearts burst around it.
  * Display:
    **"I was waiting for you. ❤️"**

---
